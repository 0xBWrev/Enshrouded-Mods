Enshrouded-Mods

My mods for Enshrouded. I make them because I want to play the game differently, and I share them in case they’re useful to someone else.

AutoLoot: picks up items without pressing E.
Mapa Deluxe: uses the illustrated Deluxe Edition map as the in-game big map.
Level and Stacks: raises the max level to 100 and increases item stack sizes.
SpeedLevelUp: level up faster.
dismantleNPC: dismantle and manage the NPCs in your base.


Each mod has its own folder with instructions. If something breaks or you have an idea, open an issue.
